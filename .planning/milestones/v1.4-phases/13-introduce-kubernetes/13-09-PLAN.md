  <action>
**Suspend Flux:** set `spec.suspend: true` on Kustomization flux-system first, then on the affected Kustomizations and HelmReleases, with `kubectl patch`. Record everything suspended.

**Workload cycle per rung**, extending the Phase 12 cycle with the new components' peak paths:
1. The established 54-request burst against both environments.
2. A nonprod full reset.
3. All three dashboards at their widest range.
4. A wide Loki query.
5. A forced reconcile of every Kustomization (requestedAt annotation).
6. A self-signed Issuer + Certificate issued and deleted in scratch namespace `ladder-scratch`, exercising cert-manager without ACME rate limits.
7. At least 20 s of settle.

**Pass criteria:**
- the container stays Ready with `restartCount` unchanged across the whole cycle;
- `lastState` shows no OOMKilled;
- `dmesg` shows no `Memory cgroup out of memory` for it;
- the component's function works (targets up, logs queryable, issuance succeeded, reconcile Ready, public health UP).

**Descent:**
- Start from each component's provisional value and halve until the first failure, stopping one rung after it.
- Stateless controllers can share rungs, since OOMKilled is reported per container: Flux ×6, cert-manager ×3, prometheus-operator, kube-state-metrics, node-exporter, Alloy, postgres-exporter.
- Prometheus, Loki and Grafana one at a time.
- Traefik last, per Task 1's timing.
- Apply rung values by patching the live Deployment/DaemonSet/StatefulSet (Flux is suspended). For Traefik, patch the kube-system Deployment directly.

**Adopt** the lowest passing rung, plus headroom where growth is expected: Prometheus and Loki carry Phase 12's day-one caveat. Take requests from each component's measured peak working set, rounded up to a 16Mi multiple.

**App/Postgres/Redpanda:** keep the limits. Set requests from `max_over_time(container_memory_working_set_bytes{namespace=~"kanban-.*",container!=""}[<since 13-06>])`, rounded up to a 64Mi multiple, and update their MEASURED comments.

**Commit:**
- Each value with a dated `MEASURED (restart-ladder, 13-09, <date>): passing rungs …, failed at …` comment.
- Flux controller resources as a strategic-merge patch file `controller-resources.yaml`, listed under `patches:` in `k8s/flux-system/kustomization.yaml`. gotk-components.yaml stays untouched.
- `invariant-checks.yml`: the gate step runs `python3 scripts/verify-k8s-invariants.py --no-provisional`.

Push, then resume Flux in reverse order. Wait until every Kustomization and HelmRelease is Ready and every pod has rolled to the committed values. Delete `ladder-scratch`.

**T0:** first `kubectl rollout restart` any workload still carrying a non-zero restartCount from before the ladder (e.g. k3s addons), so the baseline is all-zero. Once all pods are Ready and stable for 10 min, record T0 (UTC) plus a snapshot of every container's `restartCount` (`k3s kubectl get pods -A -o json` reduced to namespace/pod/container=count) in the runbook section `## k3s resource measurement — Plan 13-09 (<date>)`. Its subsections: workload, iteration ladder table (component | rungs ✓/✗ | peak at adopted | failure signal | adopted limit/request), carried values with sources, host coexistence (`free -m`, sum of requests vs allocatable), T0 + restart snapshot.
