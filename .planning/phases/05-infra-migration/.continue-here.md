<decisions_made>

- **VM shape 2 OCPU/12GB, not the 41-core trial figure.** The Service Limits page showed
  41 cores available under "Cores for StandardA1 based VM and BM instances" -- but the
  console banner confirmed this account is on a Free Trial ($300 credit) that reverts to
  Always Free limits once the trial ends. Pitfall 8 (05-RESEARCH.md / PITFALLS.md) warns
  Oracle silently halved the real Always Free A1 allocation from 4 OCPU/24GB to 2 OCPU/12GB
  in June 2026 with no announcement. Sizing against the temporary 41-core trial quota risks
  the instance being reclaimed or billed once the trial credit is exhausted. 2 OCPU/12GB
  also matches what plan 05-02 already assumed for Redpanda's `--memory`/`--smp` caps, so
  no cross-plan rework is needed.
- **Reserved IP over ephemeral.** Confirmed available at no charge on this tenancy
  (`Networking > IP Management > Reserved Public IPs` showed no cost warning). Chosen
  because the DNS A record, the SSH host fingerprint plan 05-05 pins in CI, and the OCI
  ingress rules all depend on the VM's public IP staying constant -- an ephemeral IP would
  silently break all three at once if the instance is ever recreated (a real risk on free
  tier per Pitfall 8).
- **Ubuntu 22.04+ LTS over Oracle Linux 9** (the console's default image). Matches
  STACK.md's recommendation (default `ubuntu` user, broader Docker-on-OCI documentation,
  and Docker's official apt repo is the intended install path).
- Discovered along the way: the "Always Free-eligible" badge on the OCI shape picker is
  static per shape family -- it does NOT reflect real-time quota against the tenancy, even
  when OCPU/memory sliders are pushed to their absolute platform max (80/512). Don't trust
  that badge as a quota check; use `Governance > Limits, Quotas and Usage` or the "View
  critical resources" link instead.
</decisions_made>
