## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Bare `cd` into a worktree persists | `cd <worktree> && cmd` succeeded, and every later Bash call ran there. The merge helper then compared the worktree's HEAD to itself and reported `base_mismatch`; a `cp` reported "same file". | advisory | `git -C`/absolute paths, `(cd … && …)` subshells only, and `pwd` on the first line of any call whose correctness depends on the checkout. Now in `TOOL_GOTCHAS.md` § cd. |
| A probe that cannot distinguish its own success from failure | VM `passive_opens` was chosen as the "did the SYN arrive" signal, but Docker DNAT terminates probe connections in caddy's netns, so the host counter is flat during successes AND failures. | advisory | Before trusting a correlation signal, check it moves on a known-good event. A counter that is flat when the thing it measures is present is not a signal. |
| `zsh` `=word` expansion | `echo =====X=====` errors with "not found" because zsh treats a leading `=` as an equals-expansion. | advisory | Quote separators or use `printf`. |
